About Us

Texas-Tough,
Neighbor-Honest

Lone Star Handyman was founded in 2012 by Marcus Webb, a third-generation tradesman from Fort Worth. We built this business on one principle: treat every home like it's your own mother's house.

We're not a franchise or a call center. When you call, you get Marcus. When we show up, we're on time. When we quote a price, we stick to it.
